## Who and what

Who they are, what they build, for whom, and the stage the product is at. Link the product.

## The decision

The one decision this interview is about, when it happened and what was at stake.

## How they knew

The evidence they had before deciding: what they saw, heard or measured. Ask for the messy version, not the conference talk.

## What they measured

What they watched after the change and what happened. Numbers if they can share them, direction if they cannot.

## What they would change

With hindsight, what they would do differently.

## What I take from it

What I will apply to my own products, and which atlas skill it changed my view of.
