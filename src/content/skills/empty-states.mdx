## Why it matters for a product engineer

An empty state is the screen with nothing in it yet: no projects, no results, no messages. It is often
the first screen a new user sees, and engineers often build it last, as a blank area or a bare "No
records". A good empty state says what will appear here, and how to make it appear.

## How to do it

1. Find every place that can be empty: first use, no results for a search, everything cleared, an
   error while loading.
2. Say what the status is, so people know nothing is broken.
3. Explain in one sentence what appears here and where it comes from.
4. Offer the next action directly, as a button, not as instructions to go somewhere else.
5. Consider sample data, so people can see what the product does before they add their own.
6. Make each kind of empty different. "No results for this search" needs other help than "You have no
   projects yet".

## Common mistakes

- A blank area, which looks like a bug.
- "No records" with no explanation and no next step.
- The same message for first use, an empty search and a failed load.
- A long tutorial instead of one action.

## Example

When a new Loggly account has no log data yet, its empty state offers two direct ways in: add a log
source, or explore the product with demo data. The second lets a new user see what the product does
before connecting anything.
