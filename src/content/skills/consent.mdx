## Why it matters for a product engineer

Consent is valid only when it is freely given, informed, specific and clear, and people must be able
to withdraw it. A product engineer builds the banner, the settings page and the code that decides
whether a tracking script runs. If the script runs before the choice, or the "no" is hidden, the
consent does not count. Good design here is also a product choice: tools that do not need consent,
such as privacy-friendly analytics, remove many banners.

## How to do it

1. List every cookie, script and data use, and mark which ones need consent.
2. Load nothing that needs consent until the person says yes.
3. Make "refuse" as easy as "accept": same level, same number of clicks.
4. Store each choice with a date and version, and respect it on every page and device you can.
5. Let people change their mind from a link that is always visible.

## Common mistakes

- An "Accept all" button next to a "Settings" link, with the refusal hidden inside.
- Pre-ticked boxes or "by continuing you agree" text.
- Analytics or ad tags that load before the banner answers.
- Asking for consent when a contract or another legal basis is the right one.

## Example

On 31 December 2021, the French data protection authority, the CNIL, fined Google 150 million euros
and Facebook 60 million euros. On google.fr, youtube.com and facebook.com, one click accepted all
cookies, but refusing took several. On Facebook, the last step to refuse was a button labelled "Accept
cookies". The CNIL said this pushed people toward consent. It gave both companies three months to make
refusal as easy as acceptance, or pay 100,000 euros for each day of delay.
