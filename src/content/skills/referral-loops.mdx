## Why it matters for a product engineer

A referral loop turns users into the channel: each one brings the next. It only works when the
product is worth recommending and the invitation fits the moment. The whole loop is product work:
when to ask, what the link carries, what the new person sees first, and how each step is measured.
The best loops are part of the product itself, not a page added beside it.

## How to do it

1. Ask at the moment of value, just after the product has helped, not at sign-up.
2. Choose a reward that fits the audience. Sometimes the best reward is the gift itself.
3. Make sending easy: a link that works in any app the person already uses.
4. Design what the new person sees: who sent it, what it is, and value before any sign-up.
5. Measure every step: shown, sent, opened, activated, and invited again.
6. Improve the weakest step first. A loop is only as strong as its leakiest step.

## Common mistakes

- Asking for referrals before people have seen value.
- Rewards that attract people who only want the reward.
- Measuring invitations sent, but not new users who stay.
- A landing page for invited people that asks for an account before showing anything.

## Example

RelievApp's gift appears after a session that helped. You pick a session for someone and send it
through any app; they see your name and the session plays free, with no account. The person who
receives one can give one too. Whether the loop works is not measured yet.
